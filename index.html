01Analyst Profile

I'm a cybersecurity student focused on the practical side of defense — watching systems, catching anomalies, and hardening infrastructure before it's exploited rather than after.

My work spans two connected worlds: the SOC side — SIEM tooling, log analysis, Active Directory hardening — and the DevSecOps side — baking security scanning directly into Kubernetes and Terraform pipelines so vulnerabilities get caught in CI, not in production.

I learn by building labs I can break and fix. Every project below started as a question I wanted a real, working answer to.
